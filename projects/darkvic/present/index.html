The evidence

The evidence exists, in separate feeds

Three public feeds describe the same night. None of them reports a tower that has gone silent.

Power outages show what has failed. Hazards show what may fail next. DarkVic needs both, every 15 minutes, to infer which towns have probably lost signal.

Near a hazard with no power loss is exposure, never damage: no blackout is claimed.

The solution

Reason from the evidence that survives

Everything on the map is pre-computed by this pipeline. The dashboard re-implements nothing.

What it means

Built to scale, honest about limits

Statewide is four more pollers, not a new system, and the website already invites each distributor to join.

AT SCALE

Two audiences already served: a State Control Centre intelligence view during the event, and an AusNet planning view before it.

Accessible by design: colour-blind-safe palette, adjustable text size, a guided tour and a plain-language glossary.

HONEST LIMITS

One distributor piloted: eastern Victoria only, by design.

Battery endurance is assumed, not known, and the assumption is always on screen.

Inference never carries the authority of observation. An educational prototype, not an operational system.
